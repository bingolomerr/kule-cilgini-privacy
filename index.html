Herkese açık olan bilgiler

Kullanıcı adın ve en iyi puanın, oyunu oynayan herkesin görebildiği sıralamada gösterilir. Cihaz kimliğin ve gizli anahtarın kimseye gösterilmez.

Verilerin nerede saklandığı

Sıralama verileri, veritabanı hizmeti sağlayıcımız Supabase'in (supabase.com) sunucularında saklanır. Supabase bu verileri yalnızca bizim adımıza saklar. Ayrıca ses ayarların ve skorunun bir kopyası yalnızca kendi cihazında tutulur.

Reklam, takip ve analiz

Oyunda reklam yoktur. Seni uygulamalar veya web siteleri arasında takip etmeyiz, analiz araçları kullanmayız ve verilerini kimseye satmayız ya da paylaşmayız.

Çocuklar

Oyun her yaştan oyuncuya uygundur ve bilerek çocuklardan kişisel bilgi toplamaz. Toplanan tek bilgi, oyuncunun kendi seçtiği kullanıcı adı ve skordur.

Kule Çılgını – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Kule Çılgını mobile game ("the Game") collects, why, and what we do with it. The Game is developed by bingos ("we").

Data we collect

DataWhy
The username you chooseTo show your name on the public leaderboard
Your best score and highest floor reachedTo rank you on the leaderboard
A random install ID and secret tokenSo that only your device can update your score. It is generated randomly when the Game first starts; it is not your advertising ID, serial number or any other hardware identifier.
Time of the last updateTo know when your score was last updated

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We recommend choosing a username that is not your real name.

What is public

Your username and best score appear on the leaderboard, which every player can see. Your install ID and secret token are never shown.

Where data is stored

Leaderboard data is stored on the servers of our database provider, Supabase (supabase.com), which stores it only on our behalf. Your sound settings and a copy of your score are kept only on your device.

Ads, tracking and analytics

The Game has no ads. We do not track you across apps or websites, do not use analytics tools, and do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only data collected is the username the player chooses and their score.

Deleting your data

To have your username and score removed from the leaderboard, email omer0190773@gmail.com with your username. We will delete your record within 30 days. Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.
